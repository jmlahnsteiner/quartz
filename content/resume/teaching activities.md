**Teaching activities**

- I acted as a teaching assistant for an undergraduate course on quantum field theory and an graduate course on general relativity at the University of Groningen between 2019 and 2022. Duties included design of problem sets, demonstration of problem sets, grading of homework assignments and final exams, as well as replacing the lecturer on two occasions
- I successfully completed a seminar series on teaching at university level in 2019 at the University of Groningen
- I co-supervised six BSc and two MSc projects (Jesse Knol: Supersymmetry on Maximally Symmetric Spaces and Jason Bennett: Gauge Theories) at the University of Groningen between 2019 and 2022
- I have engaged in outreach activities, among which: FameLab 2019 and 2020, RAYS for excellence 2023, Låna en forskare 2023
