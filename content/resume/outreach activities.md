I have engaged in outreach activities, among which: 

- co-organizer [THINK](https://thinkcommunity.network/); an interdisciplinary conference bringing together researcher in the natural sciences with researchers in the social sciences
- [FameLab](https://www.rug.nl/sciencelinx/famelab/?lang=en) 2019 and 2020; a competition for young researchers to present their field of expertise to a broad audience in a short time
- [RAYS for excellence](https://raysforexcellence.se/fysik) 2023 and 2024; a Swedish program giving high school students the chance to experience the research environment first-hand, did a project on Quantum Computation in 2023
- [Låna en Forskare](https://forskarfredag.se/lana-en-forskare-hall-of-fame/) 2023 and 2024; a program where high-school teachers can contact researchers to give talks in front of their pupils, contributed and gave five talks about quantum computation in 2023 and one about black hole physics in 2024
- Scientifika; »is the place to be every Friday afternoon if you want to learn more about a variety of research fields while enjoying delicious Fika! We bring together researchers across different universities in Stockholm to share their passion for science and related topics, while nourishing a deep sense of community among junior researchers.« [The abstract of my contribution can be found here. ](https://scientifika.se/talk/2024/03/15/)
